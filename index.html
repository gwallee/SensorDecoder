Which family do I have? (visual ID)

QS18 — small rectangular “wedge” body with an 18 mm threaded lens nose (fits M18 brackets). No display. The workhorse mini photoelectric.

S18-2 — smooth 18 mm threaded plastic barrel/cylinder with lock nuts, lens on the end face.

Q4X — blocky right-angle body in polished stainless steel with an angled face carrying a red digit display and 3 buttons.

Q5X — like a larger black-plastic Q4X: display + 3 buttons, bright laser spot, rotatable M12 base.

Q3X — the smallest of the display trio: compact metal-look rectangle, small red display, 2 buttons.

QS30 — mid-size rectangular WORLD-BEAM “flat-pack”, big front lens pair, much larger than QS18, no display.

Q2X — miniature flat rectangle (thumbnail-size), thin cable or M8 pigtail, no display.

Q20 — small flat low-cost rectangle, between Q2X and QS18 in size, side mounting holes, no display.
